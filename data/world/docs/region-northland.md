# Varnholt Instruments — Northland region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Northland regional office. Regional manager: Ilse Marwood.

## Overview
Several long-standing accounts renewed their service contracts, although a few asked for shorter terms while they reassess capital budgets. Freight costs were affected by a carrier surcharge introduced mid-quarter, which the logistics desk is renegotiating. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $4.820 million, compared with $5.150 million in the previous quarter. Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office.

## Customers
Sales engineers held joint visits with distributor partners to present the updated instrument range and the extended calibration plans. Spare-parts availability improved after the central depot adjusted reorder points for fast-moving items. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 3.1%, after 2.8% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. A small number of warranty claims were escalated to the central quality team for root-cause analysis; none indicated a systemic issue.

## People
Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures. The region started the quarter with 138 employees and ended it with 142 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. The regional safety committee met twice and closed all open actions from the previous inspection.

## Operations
Two technicians completed the advanced metrology certificate, bringing the number of certified staff in the region to a comfortable level. The regional team completed the migration of its field-service scheduling to the shared planning calendar, which removed a recurring source of double bookings.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
