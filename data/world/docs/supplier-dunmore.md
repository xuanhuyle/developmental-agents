# Supplier profile: Dunmore Plastics

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Dunmore Plastics supplies enclosures to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Limerick, Ireland; its main production plant, located in Ennis, opened in 2011. Sample inspections for the latest product revision passed first-article approval after one round of minor dimensional corrections. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Siobhan Keane, who succeeded Declan Rourke. The supplier's sustainability questionnaire was received and is under review by the environmental compliance office. Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate is valid until 2027-01-31. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate expired on 2025-06-30 and has not yet been renewed; a recertification audit is being scheduled. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests.

## Relationship notes
On-site visits by the supplier-quality engineers found housekeeping and traceability practices to be adequate. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team.
