# Varnholt Instruments — Westreach region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Westreach regional office. Regional manager: Dario Okonkwo.

## Overview
Sales engineers held joint visits with distributor partners to present the updated instrument range and the extended calibration plans. Freight costs were affected by a carrier surcharge introduced mid-quarter, which the logistics desk is renegotiating. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $4.475 million, compared with $4.380 million in the previous quarter. Spare-parts availability improved after the central depot adjusted reorder points for fast-moving items.

## Customers
A small number of warranty claims were escalated to the central quality team for root-cause analysis; none indicated a systemic issue. The regional safety committee met twice and closed all open actions from the previous inspection. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 5.3%, after 4.1% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures.

## People
The regional team completed the migration of its field-service scheduling to the shared planning calendar, which removed a recurring source of double bookings. The region started the quarter with 119 employees and ended it with 121 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office.

## Operations
In August a burst water main flooded part of the regional warehouse; stock was relocated within two days and insurance claims are in progress. Customer satisfaction surveys were distributed to a rotating sample of accounts, and response rates improved slightly after the reminder emails were shortened. The office lease discussion is ongoing; facilities expects a decision before the end of the fiscal year.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
