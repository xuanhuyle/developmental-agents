# Supplier profile: Brisk Components

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Brisk Components supplies connectors to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Dortmund, Germany; its main production plant, located in Bochum, opened in 2004. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Helga Brandt, who succeeded Otto Kessler. The account manager changed in the spring; the handover was documented and no open issues were lost. On-site visits by the supplier-quality engineers found housekeeping and traceability practices to be adequate.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate lapsed on 2024-09-30. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2026-11-30. The supplier indicated interest in a longer framework agreement, which procurement will evaluate during annual sourcing.

## Relationship notes
Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved. Packaging was redesigned last year to reduce damage in transit, and receiving inspection has noted fewer dented cartons since then.
