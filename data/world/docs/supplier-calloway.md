# Supplier profile: Calloway Metals

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Calloway Metals supplies housings to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1962 and is headquartered in Sheffield, United Kingdom; its main production plant, located in Rotherham, opened in 1999. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team. The supplier's sustainability questionnaire was received and is under review by the environmental compliance office.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Martin Pryce, who succeeded Alan Whitcombe. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program. Sample inspections for the latest product revision passed first-article approval after one round of minor dimensional corrections.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate is valid until 2026-06-30. The account manager changed in the spring; the handover was documented and no open issues were lost. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2027-03-31. Payment terms remain at the company standard, and there are no outstanding disputes with accounts payable.

## Relationship notes
The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation.
