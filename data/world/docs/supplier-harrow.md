# Supplier profile: Harrow Sensors

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Harrow Sensors supplies sensors to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Dayton, United States; its main production plant, located in Columbus, opened in 2018. The account manager changed in the spring; the handover was documented and no open issues were lost. Payment terms remain at the company standard, and there are no outstanding disputes with accounts payable.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Keisha Monroe, who succeeded Paul Deering. Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate is valid until 2026-09-30. The supplier indicated interest in a longer framework agreement, which procurement will evaluate during annual sourcing. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2026-05-31. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team.

## Relationship notes
On-site visits by the supplier-quality engineers found housekeeping and traceability practices to be adequate. Packaging was redesigned last year to reduce damage in transit, and receiving inspection has noted fewer dented cartons since then.
