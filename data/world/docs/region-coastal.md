# Varnholt Instruments — Coastal region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Coastal regional office. Regional manager: Bram Oyelaran.

## Overview
IT rolled out the new laptop image to the regional staff, and the helpdesk reported a modest increase in tickets during the first week. The regional team completed the migration of its field-service scheduling to the shared planning calendar, which removed a recurring source of double bookings. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $3.620 million, compared with $3.700 million in the previous quarter. The office lease discussion is ongoing; facilities expects a decision before the end of the fiscal year.

## Customers
Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office. The team piloted a new ticket triage checklist; early feedback from technicians was positive, and the checklist will be reviewed again next quarter. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 1.8%, after 2.4% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. Several long-standing accounts renewed their service contracts, although a few asked for shorter terms while they reassess capital budgets.

## People
Customer satisfaction surveys were distributed to a rotating sample of accounts, and response rates improved slightly after the reminder emails were shortened. The region started the quarter with 84 employees and ended it with 86 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. A small number of warranty claims were escalated to the central quality team for root-cause analysis; none indicated a systemic issue.

## Operations
Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures. Spare-parts availability improved after the central depot adjusted reorder points for fast-moving items.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
