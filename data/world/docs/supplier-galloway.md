# Supplier profile: Galloway Fasteners

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Galloway Fasteners supplies fasteners to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1958 and is headquartered in Hamilton, Canada; its main production plant, located in Guelph, opened in 1997. On-site visits by the supplier-quality engineers found housekeeping and traceability practices to be adequate. Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Renée Tremblay, who succeeded George Mackie. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation. Payment terms remain at the company standard, and there are no outstanding disputes with accounts payable.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate lapsed on 2024-12-31. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate expired on 2025-02-28 and has not yet been renewed; a recertification audit is being scheduled. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program.

## Relationship notes
The account manager changed in the spring; the handover was documented and no open issues were lost. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team.
