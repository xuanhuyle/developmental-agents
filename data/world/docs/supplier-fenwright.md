# Supplier profile: Fenwright Circuits

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Fenwright Circuits supplies circuit boards to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1979 and is headquartered in Eindhoven, Netherlands; its main production plant, located in Tilburg, opened in 2001. On-site visits by the supplier-quality engineers found housekeeping and traceability practices to be adequate. The account manager changed in the spring; the handover was documented and no open issues were lost.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Joost van Dael, who succeeded Marijke Smit. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate is valid until 2026-10-31. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2027-01-31. Packaging was redesigned last year to reduce damage in transit, and receiving inspection has noted fewer dented cartons since then.

## Relationship notes
The supplier indicated interest in a longer framework agreement, which procurement will evaluate during annual sourcing. Payment terms remain at the company standard, and there are no outstanding disputes with accounts payable.
