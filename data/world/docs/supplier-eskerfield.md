# Supplier profile: Eskerfield Optics

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Eskerfield Optics supplies sensors to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 2003 and is headquartered in Uppsala, Sweden; its main production plant, located in Västerås, opened in 2015. Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved. The supplier's sustainability questionnaire was received and is under review by the environmental compliance office.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Anders Holm, who succeeded Lena Varg. Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team. The supplier has been asked to provide a second-source plan for its most critical subcomponents as part of the resilience program.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate lapsed on 2025-03-31. The supplier indicated interest in a longer framework agreement, which procurement will evaluate during annual sourcing. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2026-08-31. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests.

## Relationship notes
Packaging was redesigned last year to reduce damage in transit, and receiving inspection has noted fewer dented cartons since then. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation.
