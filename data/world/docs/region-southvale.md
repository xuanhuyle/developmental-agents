# Varnholt Instruments — Southvale region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Southvale regional office. Regional manager: Rhea Lindqvist.

## Overview
The office lease discussion is ongoing; facilities expects a decision before the end of the fiscal year. Customer satisfaction surveys were distributed to a rotating sample of accounts, and response rates improved slightly after the reminder emails were shortened. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $5.310 million, compared with $5.105 million in the previous quarter. Two technicians completed the advanced metrology certificate, bringing the number of certified staff in the region to a comfortable level.

## Customers
Spare-parts availability improved after the central depot adjusted reorder points for fast-moving items. The team piloted a new ticket triage checklist; early feedback from technicians was positive, and the checklist will be reviewed again next quarter. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 4.6%, after 4.3% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. IT rolled out the new laptop image to the regional staff, and the helpdesk reported a modest increase in tickets during the first week.

## People
Sales engineers held joint visits with distributor partners to present the updated instrument range and the extended calibration plans. The region started the quarter with 160 employees and ended it with 168 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. Several long-standing accounts renewed their service contracts, although a few asked for shorter terms while they reassess capital budgets.

## Operations
Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office. Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
