# Supplier profile: Ivel Coatings

Supplier-management file maintained by Varnholt Instruments procurement. Status as of 2025-10-01.

## Company
Ivel Coatings supplies coatings to Varnholt Instruments. The company was founded in 1971 and is headquartered in Lyon, France; its main production plant, located in Grenoble, opened in 1990. Engineering change notices are exchanged through the shared portal, and turnaround on approvals has improved. The supplier participates in the quarterly business review with procurement and has generally been responsive to corrective-action requests.

## Leadership
The current chief executive officer is Camille Rousseau, who succeeded Jean Morel. The supplier indicated interest in a longer framework agreement, which procurement will evaluate during annual sourcing. Payment terms remain at the company standard, and there are no outstanding disputes with accounts payable.

## Certifications
The ISO 14001 environmental certificate lapsed on 2025-05-31. Packaging was redesigned last year to reduce damage in transit, and receiving inspection has noted fewer dented cartons since then. Its ISO 9001 quality-management certificate is valid until 2026-12-31. Lead times were stable over the review period, with occasional delays attributed to upstream raw-material allocation.

## Relationship notes
Forecast sharing now happens monthly, which has reduced the number of expedite requests from the planning team. Sample inspections for the latest product revision passed first-article approval after one round of minor dimensional corrections.
