# Varnholt Instruments — Midlands region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Midlands regional office. Regional manager: Priya Castellanos.

## Overview
The regional team completed the migration of its field-service scheduling to the shared planning calendar, which removed a recurring source of double bookings. Customer satisfaction surveys were distributed to a rotating sample of accounts, and response rates improved slightly after the reminder emails were shortened. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $4.105 million, compared with $3.990 million in the previous quarter. The office lease discussion is ongoing; facilities expects a decision before the end of the fiscal year.

## Customers
Several long-standing accounts renewed their service contracts, although a few asked for shorter terms while they reassess capital budgets. A small number of warranty claims were escalated to the central quality team for root-cause analysis; none indicated a systemic issue. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 2.9%, after 2.6% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. The regional safety committee met twice and closed all open actions from the previous inspection.

## People
Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures. The region started the quarter with 112 employees and ended it with 110 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. Freight costs were affected by a carrier surcharge introduced mid-quarter, which the logistics desk is renegotiating.

## Operations
Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office. IT rolled out the new laptop image to the regional staff, and the helpdesk reported a modest increase in tickets during the first week.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
