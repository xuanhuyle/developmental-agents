# Varnholt Instruments — Eastmarch region: quarterly report, 2025-Q3

Prepared by the Eastmarch regional office. Regional manager: Tomasz Adeyemi.

## Overview
The office lease discussion is ongoing; facilities expects a decision before the end of the fiscal year. Warehouse cycle counts were performed on schedule and variances remained within the tolerance agreed with the finance office. The quarter closed with regional revenue of $3.950 million, compared with $4.020 million in the previous quarter. Several long-standing accounts renewed their service contracts, although a few asked for shorter terms while they reassess capital budgets.

## Customers
Sales engineers held joint visits with distributor partners to present the updated instrument range and the extended calibration plans. The regional safety committee met twice and closed all open actions from the previous inspection. Customer churn for the quarter came in at 2.2%, after 3.5% in 2025-Q2; the regional target remains below 3.0%. Freight costs were affected by a carrier surcharge introduced mid-quarter, which the logistics desk is renegotiating.

## People
A small number of warranty claims were escalated to the central quality team for root-cause analysis; none indicated a systemic issue. The region started the quarter with 101 employees and ended it with 97 employees, after planned transfers and a small number of new hires. Training hours per employee were broadly in line with the company guideline, with most sessions focused on the updated calibration procedures.

## Operations
Two technicians completed the advanced metrology certificate, bringing the number of certified staff in the region to a comfortable level. The regional team completed the migration of its field-service scheduling to the shared planning calendar, which removed a recurring source of double bookings.

## Outlook
The regional plan for the next quarter emphasises service-contract renewals and continued attention to response times. Figures in this report are preliminary until confirmed by the central finance team.
